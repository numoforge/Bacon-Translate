    History

      Privacy

      Sent to Google: the text you translate, the chosen languages, and, as with any web request, your IP address and browser details. No referrer is sent. Google's own privacy policy applies to that data.

      Not collected by us: Bacon Translate has no backend, no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no API keys. We never receive your text.

      Stored on your device only: your translation history (up to 50 items), language choices and theme, in your browser's local storage. Turn history off or delete it in the History panel at any time.

      Hosting: the page is served by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy policy.

      Limitation: this app uses Google's unofficial, keyless web endpoint. It is undocumented, may be rate-limited, and may change or stop working without notice.
